## Camera Dock implementation (2026-10-16)

Camera Dock is an opt-in placement of the existing floating presentation controller. CueFlowCore produces a single layout consumed by the live panel and the placement map in the Camera Dock inspector. The map preserves the display's aspect ratio, shows the menu-bar band and camera housing, and distinguishes the full black backing from the usable prompt content. The editor's main stage remains a live prompt preview while Camera Dock is active. Captions distinguish integrated notch placement from built-in no-inset and external fallbacks.

On a built-in display with a safe-area inset, the non-activating panel is anchored to the physical display top; its opaque black backing joins the hardware notch while prompt content begins below the safe edge. It has square top corners, rounded 14 pt lower corners, no shadow, and a window level above the menu bar while active. When the safe inset is absent but two valid, top-aligned auxiliary areas exist, CueGlass infers their shared safe-band height; incomplete or inconsistent metadata keeps the honest fallback placement. No-inset and external fallbacks sit 6 pt below `visibleFrame.maxY`, use near-black with 14 pt corners, and have a subtle shadow. All styles remain fixed in place and join Spaces/full-screen applications.

Committing from Preview, Floating, or Output captures the anchor, prior surface, and prior play state, pauses the single shared session, closes the previous surface, and shows a visible paused Ready Dock. Play starts playback. Exiting restores the captured surface and play state. The inspector uses a nonmodal first-use callout and persistent capture disclosure. It says the prompt may appear in screen recordings and full-display shares; no single-app window-sharing claim is made. CueGlass does not access the camera or microphone for Camera Dock. On external fallback with a built-in display connected, the grip indicates that the camera may be on another display. A vanished display or invalid fit pauses and closes the Dock while retaining the position; it does not choose another surface automatically.

## Follow Voice microphone continuity and catch-up glide (2026-10-17)

With an external microphone attached, the owner saw Follow Voice lose the microphone and lag behind speech. Cause one: the capture source treated every `AVAudioEngineConfigurationChange` (sample-rate or channel-count change when a microphone is plugged, unplugged, or switched) as fatal and ended the session with "The audio input device changed or disconnected."
